How long do you hold a reserved table?

We hold tables for 15 minutes past your reservation time. Give us a quick call at (208) 333 9296 if you are running late.

Can I request a specific seating area?

Yes—let us know your preference for window tables, quiet corners, or group seating in the Your Message box above.

Do you welcome walk-in guests?

Yes, we always reserve a portion of our café and bar seating for walk-in guests alongside advance bookings.

What are your dining hours?

Mon–Thu 10:30AM–9:30PM, Friday 10:00AM–10:30PM, and Sat–Sun 09:30AM–11:00PM.
